## Product positioning

Workmate AI is an AI-powered workplace productivity assistant. Its required workplace modes are Smart Email, AI Research Assistant, and AI Workplace Chat. The Resume & Career Assistant is the differentiated feature.

## Advanced workflow

Resume workflow:
User input -> generation -> claim validation -> AI evaluation -> up to two revisions -> final evaluation -> human review.

## Grounding

Job-description keyword analysis is based on the text supplied by the user. Missing keywords are not evidence that the candidate has the corresponding skill.

## Responsible AI

The system must avoid invented qualifications, employers, dates, achievements, certifications, skills, sources, statistics and test results. External/pasted content is treated as data, not instructions, to reduce prompt-injection risk.

## Testing

Five synthetic evaluation cases are defined. They remain NOT TESTED until a real Claude run is completed. Productivity improvements are also NOT CLAIMED until measured.
